Frequently asked questions

How do I book a viewing?

Send us the property you're interested in, your name, your phone number and two or three times that suit you. An agent will confirm on WhatsApp. Viewings run Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm.

Which documents do I need to buy?

A valid CNIC (or NICOP for overseas Pakistanis), two passport-size photos and proof of payment. Some societies ask for more; your agent will give you the full list.

How much deposit is needed to rent?

Usually one or two months' rent as security, plus one month's rent in advance. Rental agreements are normally 11 months and can be renewed.

Do you arrange home loans?

No, but we can share contact details of banks our past clients have used.

Do you deal in plot files?

No. For safety reasons, we only deal in properties with a confirmed plot number and possession.

Do you cover other cities?

No, we only handle properties in Lahore.
